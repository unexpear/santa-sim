# GADGETS.md — Elf Gadget Ideas

> **Rule change (the designer, Oct 20): Snooze Darts.** The exit does NOT lock. You get 10 darts for the whole run, and they never regenerate. Picking a dart back up returns it to your supply. The Dart Magnet only grabs the specific dart you're looking at (crosshair, line of sight) within 20 elf heights, one at a time. Leaving a dart behind still exposes you: there's a warning at exit and a newspaper/penalty flag.


> Inspiration source: **Disney's *Prep & Landing* (2009 TV special, Walt Disney Animation Studios, ABC, 22 min)**.
> Scope: **only the original 2009 special** (per the designer). The sequels/shorts are deliberately excluded.
> The designer's reference image (two small elves with glowing green night-vision goggles in a fireplace, stockings above) matches the
> special's look and its DVD menu art ("two elves in green night vision goggles peeking out of the chimney", High Def Universe review).
> We did not do a reverse image search, so it's a strong match rather than a 100% confirmed frame.

**Legal note:** this is a commercial game. We take *inspiration* only: mechanics and vibes. No show names, character names,
call signs, code words or gadget names go into the game. Every gadget below has our own name, and our designs should
look different (summer Aussie theme, our own silhouettes). Don't use show terms like "Prep & Landing", "Gingerbread Man/GBM",
"Figgy Pudding", "sparkle", "Big Guy", "Thrasher", or "creatures stirring" anywhere in game text or assets.

Legend: ⭐ = **prototype pick** (cheap to build + core to the loop)

## 1. What the elves use in the 2009 special → our version

| # | In the show (1 line) | Our renamed version | Game mechanic (fits our rules) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Night-vision goggles**: green-glowing goggles worn for the whole indoor op. | ⭐ **Glow-Gogs** | Toggle (N). Dark rooms become readable, and residents, darts and pets show as bright silhouettes. The catch is that the goggles' own green glow can be spotted: residents facing you notice you 25% faster while they're on. Bright lights (open fridge, TV, phone screens) flare the view, so turn them off near light sources. |
| 2 | **Candy-cane grappling hook / zip line**: a cane whose crook pops into a 3-prong hook, fires a line (~30 ft) and retracts; used to get up/down the chimney. | ⭐ **Crook-Line** (a stripy hook shaped like a beach-umbrella handle) | The chimney entry/exit tool: abseil down, winch up. Indoors it also does short grapples onto shelves, bench tops and bunk beds (the elf is knee-high, so furniture is terrain). The line is visible and makes a whirr, so firing it in a resident's view is risky. Limited cable length per shot. |
| 3 | **Fireplace fire put out on arrival**: a roaring fire is quickly extinguished before they climb out. | **Hearth Snuffer** | Aussie summer means most fireplaces are cold. Some houses have a **barbecue-flue chimney** or a fire pit, plus one cold-snap Tasmania level. If it's lit, use the Snuffer before you drop down or you take soot damage and puff smoke into the room (a noise and visibility spike). |
| 4 | **Multi-tool "decoration" handheld**: hides a touchscreen with a stirring-creature scanner, list lookup, cookie analysis, gift-clearance hologram, flashlight and media player. | ⭐ **Snag-Pad** (shaped like a sausage-sizzle snag/ornament, our own silhouette) | The elf's main HUD gadget. Each app below is separate so they can be unlocked over time: |
| 4a | ↳ **Creature scanner**: tells whether anyone in the house is awake. | ⭐ **Snore-o-Meter** | Ping shows every resident's sleep depth (Deep / Light / Restless / AWAKE) and the pets. Pings take a few seconds to recharge. Sleep gets lighter as the night goes on, which fits our "residents get more active" difficulty curve. |
| 4b | ↳ **Naughty/Nice list lookup**: profile and recent behaviour of each child in the house. | ⭐ **The Ledger** | This is our "check the list" task. Scan each child's bedroom door or name tag to confirm their status, and the right present in the sleigh stack gets tagged for them. If you skip it, you can mix up gifts (wrong-gift grade penalty). It also has a "Deeds" mini-feed for the newspaper ("local boy who fed neighbour's dog gets bike"). |
| 4c | ↳ **Cookie analysis scanner**: checks the treats left out (Santa hates nuts). | **Snack Sniffer** | This is our "eat the cookies" task. Scan the plate, which might hold Anzac bikkies, lamingtons, a beer and a carrot for the reindeer. A bad snack (nuts, Vegemite surprise) gives a short "burp" noise risk if you eat it anyway. Leaving the plate untouched costs grade, because kids notice. |
| 4d | ↳ **Gift-clearance hologram**: projects a scale ghost of the present under the tree to check it fits. | ⭐ **Fit-Ghost** | This is our present placement. Hold to show a hologram of the top sleigh present at the tree. Green = fits, red = it'll knock baubles off (noise and a mess). This ties straight into sleigh packing: you see what's next on the stack before you commit. |
| 4e | ↳ **Flashlight (strobe/multicolour)** | **Torch Mode** | A dim red torch for reading labels. A strobe flash can be used as a *distraction* in the next room. It's never pointed at a sleeping face, because that wakes them. |
| 4f | ↳ **Partner sync / remote control** | **Buddy Link** | Later co-op or two-elf mode: one elf drives the other's Snag-Pad (a lookout feeds the pinger while the runner places gifts). |
| 5 | **Snowflake buzzsaw (wrist)**: telescopes out to trim tree branches when a gift won't fit. | **Wattle Trimmer** (a wrist saw shaped like a wattle flower) | When the Fit-Ghost is red, trim branches quietly in short bursts. Each burst adds noise, and too much trimming is a "someone vandalised our tree" newspaper line. Some trees are fake plastic ones, which are louder to cut. |
| 6 | **Angel air gun**: a tree-topper angel that's secretly a compressed-air canister used to fluff stockings ("hung with care"); also a shredder. | ⭐ **Puff-Possum** (a possum ornament air canister) | This is our "fill stockings" task. Puff the stocking open, then lob the small gifts in. The **shredder** mode destroys *evidence*: wrapping scraps, a dart's packaging, or a kid's "SANTA TRAP" note. Fun extra: shredding a kid's Santa-trap blueprint disarms the trap. |
| 7 | **Sleep "sparkle" ornament**: a bauble that dissolves ~3 s after its hook is twisted; a well-timed throw puts anyone (a dog in the opening, the kid later) to sleep ~1 hour. | ⭐ **Snooze Darts** (ours, already in DESIGN.md) + **Drowse Bauble** (thrown area version) | Darts are our core tool. The **Drowse Bauble** variant is a thrown bauble with a ~3 s fuse. Throw it too early and it pops mid-air, a dud with noise. Throw it too late and it bursts in your hand, and the *elf* naps 5 s. **Our rule still applies:** a bauble leaves glitter residue, so you must vacuum or collect it like a dart or it counts as evidence ("DRUGGED WITH GLITTER?" headline). Sleep time scales with body weight, just like in the show, so the dog sleeps longer than Dad. |
| 8 | **Kid wakes up and photographs the elf** (Timmy's toy camera): the elves have to deal with a witness. | ⭐ **Evidence Sweep** (technique, not a gadget) | If a resident wakes and sees you, they're *spotted* (seen = robbed 10x + Stingy Jack curse), **unless** you dart them within a short grace window **and** recover any photo they took. The phone/camera becomes an evidence item like a dart: wipe or pocket it before you leave. This gives a seen-but-saved clutch play. |
| 9 | **Rooftop landing-light kit** (disguised snowman decoration): springs apart into weighted pieces, a dish signals the sleigh, the pieces and tubing strobe to make a landing strip, and the elves' units link a **cable that catches the sleigh's tailhook**. | ⭐ **Strip Kit** (a disguised garden-gnome/flamingo set, our own design) | This links the elf phase to our roof landing. Before leaving a house, place the kit on the *next* house's roof (or the elves deploy it on arrival), and it lays our colour-coded string-light guides: **green** safe, **blue** slick (rain in Australia), **red** steep/danger, **gold** bonus. A deployed kit adds an **arrestor cable**: snag it with the sleigh's hook for an extra-gentle stop, a bonus star or rescue on slick roofs. If you don't place it, you land on the house's own lights only (harder). |
| 10 | **Sleigh waved off in a storm** (abort code) because the house wasn't prepped. | ⭐ **Wave-Off** (an abort call we name ourselves, e.g. "BUSHFIRE BLANKET" / "PASS THE PAV") | A timer per house: if the elves haven't finished prep (lights placed, residents safe) by the time the sleigh is due, mission control calls a wave-off and the house is **skipped** (a grade hit and a newspaper story: "SANTA SKIPPED NO. 12 GUMNUT CRES"). The player can choose to **override** the call and land anyway in bad weather, which is high risk and high reward, like the show's climax. |
| 11 | **Improvised landing strip from neighbours' decorations and an inflatable snow-globe Santa** after the real device is lost in the storm. | **Borrowed Lights** | If the Strip Kit is lost (dropped off the roof, broken), grab nearby string lights and inflatables from the street to build a makeshift strip. Every light you take has to be **returned** before dawn or it's a newspaper theft story ("GRINCH STEALS FAIRY LIGHTS"). This mirrors our dart rule. |
| 12 | **Mission control / command centre**: tracks weather, every house prep status and the sleigh's flight; radios the elves via headset. | ⭐ **Mission Desk + Earpiece** | Radio VO from a mission-control character of our own (an outback flying-doctor-style controller). Gives weather (rain = blue/slick roofs, gusts), the next house, and a sleigh ETA countdown. The earpiece also warns "resident stirring in bedroom 2" when your Snore-o-Meter is on cooldown. This is cheap to build: text + a countdown at first. |
| 13 | **Call signs and radio etiquette**: each elf, the sleigh and the reindeer have Christmas-carol call signs. | **Our call signs** (Aussie carol / bush themed: "Kookaburra", "Jolly Swagman", "Six White Boomers"-style; avoid real song lyrics we don't own) | Flavour only. Used in radio barks and newspaper quotes ("a source who identified only as 'Kookaburra'"). |
| 14 | **Covert transport reindeer**: a stealth reindeer that drops the elf team at houses ahead of Santa (shaved antlers, black-painted underside). | **Night Runner** (a stealth reindeer, our design: a dark-coat bush reindeer with taped antlers) | Explains why elves arrive *before* the sleigh. Later mode: fly the advance team in a fast, fragile scout run to place Strip Kits on several roofs before Santa. |
| 15 | **Milk temperature check**: one of the prep gadgets (noted in reviews). | **Milk Probe** → in Australia, **"Is the beer cold?"** | A tiny optional snack-task: check the drink. A warm beer for Santa gives a grade hit, and you can swap it for a cold one from the fridge, but opening the fridge light flares your Glow-Gogs (see #1). |
| 16 | **Motto: "get in, get out, never be noticed"** | **Our motto** e.g. *"In by the chimney, out by the chimney, nobody the wiser."* | This is the design pillar for the indoor phase. Score categories map to it: **Time** (get in/out), **Tasks** (stockings, presents, list, snack) and **Ghost** (no sightings, no evidence left: darts, glitter, borrowed lights, photos). |

## 2. Original gadget ideas (ours, same spirit, summer Australia)

| # | Gadget | What it does / mechanic | Prototype? |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | ⭐ **Dart Magnet** (a reel-in thimble) | Pulls nearby spent darts back to the elf within a short radius. It makes the "never leave a dart" rule playable without being tedious. The HUD shows a **Dart Count: fired / recovered** that must match before the chimney exit unlocks (or you leave knowingly and get exposed). | ⭐ yes |
| O2 | ⭐ **Tuck Trolley** (a fold-out roller skateboard) | Carrying a darted adult as a knee-high elf is slow. The trolley lets you roll them back to bed faster, but it's noisy on floorboards and quiet on carpet. Ties into the bed-tucking rule. | ⭐ yes |
| O3 | **Floorboard Map** | A Snag-Pad app that marks creaky boards (red) after a ping. Aussie houses (old Queenslanders on stumps) creak a lot. | later |
| O4 | **Cicada Caller** | Throwable noise lure that plays cicada or possum-on-the-roof sounds to pull a resident's attention or send the dog to the back door. It's very Australian, and residents ignore it after 2 uses per house. | maybe |
| O5 | **Fan Freezer** | Every summer house has a ceiling fan or aircon. Freeze the fan so it stops blowing papers and wrapping off the table, or turn the aircon UP so residents sleep deeper (deeper Snore-o-Meter levels for 60 s). Leaving it changed is a small "weird" clue in the paper. | later |
| O6 | **Mozzie Net Cloak** | Fly-screen camo poncho. Standing still against flyscreens, curtains or dark corners makes you near-invisible, but moving drops it. A cheap stealth-crouch alternative. | maybe |
| O7 | **Pet Pacifier** | Dog/cat/cockatoo handling. Give it a treat or a scratch to keep the pet calm. A cockatoo that sees you will *repeat* noises later (a delayed alarm). | later |
| O8 | **Thongs-Off Socks** | Silent-step booties. Wear them for quiet movement, but they're slippery on tiles (a slide risk, mirroring blue slick roofs). | maybe |
| O9 | **Gift Lift** (a mini pulley) | Lowers presents from the chimney to the floor in sleigh-stack order. If you call down the wrong present it's a time penalty (our packing rule indoors). It can also hoist one fallen present back up. | later |
| O10 | **Pre-Dawn Clock** | A wristwatch showing time to sunrise. Australian summer sunrise is early (~5:45 am), so the last houses of the night are the tightest. | cheap, later |

## 3. Prototype picks (first indoor stealth test)

Cheap to build, and each one exercises a core rule:

1. **Glow-Gogs** (night-vision toggle, a post-process tint we already planned on N) + the "goggle glow can be noticed" detection modifier.
2. **Crook-Line**: chimney down/up only for v1 (no free grappling yet).
3. **Snore-o-Meter** ping: per-resident sleep state on the HUD.
4. **Snooze Darts + Dart Magnet + Dart Count gate**: the hard "never leave a dart" rule, made playable.
5. **Tuck Trolley** / carry-to-bed: one darted adult has to go back to bed.
6. **Fit-Ghost** present placement under the tree + **Puff-Possum** stocking fill (one tree, one stocking).
7. **The Ledger**: scan one child's door and get the right gift.
8. **Evidence Sweep / exit check**: chimney exit summarises Darts ✓, Photos ✓, Borrowed items ✓. Then go to the newspaper.

Next up after the prototype: **Strip Kit** (connects the elf phase to roof landing via the colour-coded lights), **Wave-Off** timer, and the **Mission Desk** radio.
